Biography

John W. Mullan is a tenor and ensemble singer based in Washington, D.C. He performs with Ars Populi, the Choir of the Basilica of the National Shrine of the Immaculate Conception, The Thirteen, and the Washington National Cathedral Choir.

Highlights of the 2026–27 season include Bach’s St. Matthew Passion with The Thirteen at Washington National Cathedral, a joint concert with Siglo de Oro, and the Robert White Lamentations with Ars Populi. Recent seasons have included Handel’s Messiah and Bach’s Magnificat at Washington National Cathedral, a performance tour to Rome with the Choir of the Basilica of the National Shrine, and Palestrina’s Missa Tu Es Petrus with Ars Populi.

He studies voice with Elizabeth Daniels.
